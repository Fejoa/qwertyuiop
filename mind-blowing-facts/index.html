Could Saturn float in a giant bathtub?

It sounds like a cosmic prank. The science behind it is even cooler.

Saturn's average density is lower than water's, so in a wildly imaginary bathtub big enough for the planet, it would theoretically float. No such bathtub could actually exist in practice!

Octopuses have three hearts

Two help move blood through the gills. The third pumps it around the body.

Octopus blood is also blue because it uses a copper-containing protein called haemocyanin to carry oxygen.

A day on Venus is longer than its year

Venus spins so slowly that one rotation takes longer than one trip around the Sun.

One Venus rotation takes about 243 Earth days; its orbit around the Sun takes about 225 Earth days. These are measured relative to distant stars.

Lightning can be hotter than the Sun's surface

A lightning channel can briefly reach around 30,000°C.

That's roughly five times the temperature of the Sun's visible surface. Lightning heats air extremely quickly, making it expand and creating thunder.

Your brain tissue has no pain receptors

The brain processes pain, but brain tissue itself cannot directly feel pain.

Pain-sensitive structures such as the scalp, blood vessels and membranes around the brain can still hurt. That's one reason headaches are real even though brain tissue lacks pain receptors.

Fungi are more closely related to animals than plants

Mushrooms may look plant-like, but their evolutionary family tree tells a different story.

Fungi and animals share a more recent common ancestor with each other than either group does with plants. Fungi absorb nutrients rather than photosynthesising.

Looking into space is looking into the past

Light takes time to travel, so distant objects appear as they were long ago.

Sunlight takes about 8 minutes 20 seconds to reach Earth. Light from a galaxy billions of light-years away began its journey billions of years ago.
SCIENCE
🧊

Hot water can sometimes freeze faster than cold water

This surprising observation is known as the Mpemba effect.

It doesn't happen in every setup. Evaporation, convection, dissolved gases and the conditions of the experiment can affect the result; scientists still discuss exactly when and why it occurs.
HUMAN BODY
🩸

Your blood isn't blue inside your veins

Blood is red, although veins can look bluish through skin.

Blood carrying less oxygen is a darker red, not blue. The way light interacts with skin and tissue makes some veins appear blue.
NATURE
🌱

Plants and fungi can exchange resources

Many plant roots partner with fungi in a relationship called mycorrhiza.

Fungal threads can help plants access water and minerals, while plants provide sugars. Scientists study how much resources move between plants through these networks; popular claims about forest-wide communication can be overstated.
