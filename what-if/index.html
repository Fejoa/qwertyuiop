Questioning the existence of humans? Read this compelling book.

Thiaoouba Prophecy · Michel Desmarquet

Thiaoouba Prophecy presents the author’s account of an extraordinary alleged encounter and journey involving an advanced extraterrestrial civilisation. It explores big, unsettling ideas about where humanity came from, whether life exists beyond Earth, and how we understand our place in the universe. Read it as a provocative speculative account that invites questions—not as a scientific textbook.
